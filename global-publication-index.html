HANDWRITING PROJECT — INFORMATION & GUIDELINES

This document is a general information checklist for anyone considering a handwriting project. Read the project-specific terms and obtain written confirmation before beginning.

2. Before registration or starting work

  • Ask for the complete project description, sample pages and written acceptance criteria.
  • Confirm exact page count, one-side or both-side writing, paper size, ink colour and correction rules.
  • Get start date, final submission date, delivery method and responsible representative's contact details in writing.
  • Ask for a written breakdown of every fee, its purpose, refund policy and receipt details. Do not pay unexplained charges or act under pressure.

3. Payment and earnings

  • Obtain total payment amount, approval conditions, payment milestones and expected payment date in writing.
  • If an advance is offered, confirm its exact percentage, timing and conditions in a written agreement before starting.
  • Advertised income ranges are not guaranteed earnings. Actual payment may depend on approved work and the written agreement.
  • Keep copies of receipts, messages, agreements and submitted-work acknowledgements.

4. Writing and delivery checklist

  • Follow the approved sample and instructions consistently.
  • Keep pages clean, legible, complete and in the required order.
  • Check spelling, page count and corrections before packing or submitting.
  • Use a trackable delivery method if physical pages are requested and retain the tracking receipt.

5. Verification and transparency

  • Verify business identity, contact details and project terms independently before sharing documents or paying any fee.
  • Do not treat this page as proof of government approval, government verification, certification or guaranteed employment.
  • Ask for valid supporting documents if any approval, registration or certification claim is made.
Important notice: This is a general guideline, not a contract, proof of company registration, government endorsement, guaranteed work, guaranteed income or promise of payment. Confirm all terms directly in writing before participation.
